      Keep LLM egress in ms-system-setting, but move the two refusal decisions to the edge: the gateway serves the whole `/ai/conversation/*` subtree with 503 when `AI_ENABLED` is off and pre-checks the organisation's AI budget against Redis counters (reusing `internal/ratelimit` and `rediskeys`) before proxying. ms-system-setting keeps the conversation store, `ResolveModel` and the `aiclient` call, and emits usage events through the outbox that a consumer folds into an append-only ledger.

      Same placement as opt-1 but with the smallest new surface: `AI_ENABLED` and the AI limits become `system_parameter` policy keys read through `shared/pkg/policy` (database over environment, admin-editable, cached), consumption is derived from `tokens_in/tokens_out` already stored on `ai_conversation_message` plus a monthly rollup row, cost is estimated from a static price table, and model scoping uses a small `ai_model_scope` join table instead of new columns. Per-project overrides and money-accurate cost are a follow-up.
  why recommended: It is the design the repository has already reviewed for T-F3-18 (SOL-P3c-E §3.2) and it respects the settled placement of LLM egress in ms-system-setting, so almost nothing has to be invented.
